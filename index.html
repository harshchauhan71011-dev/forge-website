How we build

Four stages, same as any forge.

A blacksmith doesn't skip steps to save time — skipping a step is how steel cracks later. We run every build through the same four stages, in order.

01 / Heat

Discovery

We find out what the site actually has to do, for whom, and what it costs you if it fails — before a single pixel gets placed.

02 / Hammer

Build

The site takes shape in fast, visible passes. You see real structure early, not a locked comp six weeks in.

03 / Temper

Refine

Hardened steel gets reheated slightly so it won't shatter under stress. We do the same: performance, copy, and edge cases, worked over until they hold.

04 / Quench

Launch

The rapid cool that locks the hardness in. We ship, watch it under real traffic, and stay on for the first round of fixes.

Temper colors — the visible readout of heat in steel, from pale straw near 200°C to deep blue near 300°C. Blacksmiths read them to know exactly when to stop. We borrowed the palette; you're welcome to borrow the discipline.
